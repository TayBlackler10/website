Coach Te.Strength Coach and Personal Trainer in Grafton

Te Ao Kura Ormsby, Te for short, has represented Waikato and New Zealand in sport. He helps people build strength and muscle, lose fat, and move and recover well.

Email Te Book your free PT session
Coaches Strength Club, Mon, Wed and Fri at 6:00amM2, 8 Nugent Street, Grafton
Who Te helps

Stronger, and moving well

Whether you want to build muscle, drop some fat or just feel better in your body, Te builds the plan around you.

Strength

Get stronger on the big lifts with good technique and a plan that builds week to week.

Muscle building

Structured training to help you put on muscle and keep it.

Fat loss

Training that helps you lose fat while you keep getting stronger.

Mobility and recovery

Moving well and recovering properly. Te knows firsthand how much this matters.

About Te

A lifetime of sport

Te has represented Waikato in cricket, tennis, volleyball and touch rugby, and New Zealand in badminton.

A serious spinal injury in 2025 showed him firsthand how much movement and structured recovery matter. That shapes how he coaches. Getting stronger is the goal, and looking after your body along the way is part of it.

You'll also find Te on the floor coaching Strength Club on Monday, Wednesday and Friday mornings at 6:00am.

How it works

Three simple steps

1 Book a free sessionNew members get a free PT session. Already a member? Just email Te.
2 Meet and planTe finds out where you're at and what you want, then builds a plan around it.
3 TrainYou train together at M2 in Grafton, and the plan moves forward as you do.
