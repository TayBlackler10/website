About Joe

From the football pitch to the boxing ring

Joe started on the football pitch at eight. In the British Army he took up boxing, competed as an amateur and coached it in his regiment.

These days he coaches at M2 in Grafton. He loves working with beginners who aren't sure where to start, and he'll meet you wherever you're at.

Qualifications
  • CIMSPA Level 3 Personal Trainer
  • ASCA Level 1 Strength and Conditioning
  • England Boxing Level 2
  • Nutrition coaching
