Hydrotherapy

Why recovery matters

🧚
Cold Water Immersion

Cold water constricts then dilates blood vessels, flushing out what makes you sore. Regular exposure can reduce next-day soreness by up to 20%.

🔥
Heat Therapy

Heat increases blood flow to muscles and improves sleep quality — both directly affect recovery and performance.

🔄
Contrast Therapy

Alternating ice bath and sauna forces blood vessels to rapidly open and close, speeding up muscle repair.

💧
Active Recovery Swimming

Easy swimming reduces swelling and keeps circulation going and joints mobile without adding load.

Recovery protocol

Getting the most from recovery.

1
Post-Training (0–30 min)

Get in the ice bath within 30 minutes of finishing, while inflammation is highest.

2
Contrast Protocol

3 min ice bath → 10 min sauna. Repeat 2–3 rounds. Finish cold for recovery, warm for relaxation.

3
Consistency Is Key

Two or three recovery sessions a week makes a noticeable difference within a month or two.

4
Active Recovery Days

Use the 20m pool for easy swims on rest days — 20–30 mins of easy freestyle.

Recovery FAQs

Is the recovery area included in all memberships?

The full recovery area — ice bath, sauna, spa pool and 20m pool — is included in the Perform membership. Fitness Passport members at M2 also receive full Perform benefits.

Do I need to book in advance?

No booking required. All recovery facilities are first-come, first-served. Open Mon–Fri 5am–10pm and Sat–Sun 7am–7pm.

How cold is the ice bath?

Maintained at approximately 8–12°C — the optimal range for cold therapy benefits. Start with 2–3 minute sessions and build up gradually.

Do Fitness Passport members get recovery access?

Yes — Fitness Passport members at M2 receive full Perform membership benefits, including unlimited access to the ice bath, sauna, spa pool and 20m pool.
