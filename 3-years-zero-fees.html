How it works

Three years of M2. Here's our thanks.

M2 opened in Grafton in October 2023. To celebrate, we're taking away every reason not to start.

1 Pick a membership aboveThe 3YEARS code is added for you. Join online in a few minutes, any time up to Sunday 18 October.
2 Pick up your key tagCome in to reception. Your key tag is free with this offer.
3 Train free for 4 weeksYour normal weekly price starts after your first 4 weeks. Every membership except Recovery also gets a free session with a coach.
Good to know

Common questions

What do I actually pay?
Nothing for your first 4 weeks. No joining fee and no key tag fee either. After 4 weeks your membership carries on at its normal price.
Is there a minimum term?
Yes, the normal one. Perform, Classes and Recovery are 6 months, Daily is 12 months. Want no lock-in? Pick the Flexi option on any card: $5 a week more, with 30 days' notice to cancel.
I've been a member before. Can I use it?
Yes. Welcome back. Join through the same link and the offer applies.
I'm on Fitness Passport. Does this apply?
No, M2 offers don't apply to Fitness Passport memberships. Your visits are already covered through work. How Fitness Passport works at M2
I'm already a member?
This one's for new joiners, but you can still get 4 weeks free by bringing a mate. See Bring a Mate
Can I just come and try it first?
Yes. Anyone can train free during Birthday Open Week, 12 to 18 October. Join before Sunday night to keep the 4 free weeks.

Offer ends 11:59pm Sunday 18 October 2026. For new and returning members joining on a weekly, fortnightly or monthly Perform, Classes, Daily or Recovery membership. First 4 weeks free, then the normal price and minimum term apply. Joining fee and key tag fee waived. Not available on Fitness Passport, corporate or paid in full memberships, and can't be combined with other offers. One per person.
