Good to know

Annual questions

How is the annual rate paid?
One upfront payment, nothing further. You're locked in for 12 months from the date you start.
Can I cancel an annual membership?
Annual memberships are paid in full upfront. If your circumstances change, email reception@m2club.co.nz and we'll do our best to help.
Why are spots limited?
We limit annual spots to keep it fair. Once they're gone, these rates close and standard weekly pricing applies.
Can I upgrade from Daily to Perform?
Yes. Talk to us and we'll sort out a pro-rata upgrade based on the time left on your current plan.
