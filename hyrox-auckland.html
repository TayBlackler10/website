Good to know

Common questions

When is HYROX Auckland?
4 to 7 February 2027. M2 is an official HYROX Training Club in Grafton, so it's a good place to get ready for it.
How do I claim the membership offer?
Bring proof of your HYROX Auckland race entry to reception at 8 Nugent Street, Grafton. You'll get Perform Flexi for $35 a week instead of $54.50. It's only available in person and can't be claimed online.
What counts as proof of my race entry?
Your HYROX Auckland registration confirmation, whether that's an email, a screenshot or your entry showing in the HYROX app.
Is the 2 week plan really free?
Yes, no cost and no catch. Pop in your name and email and the plan opens straight away.
Can I get a personalised program instead?
Yes. Dave and Bekka both offer online HYROX coaching at a discount for anyone racing HYROX Auckland. Get in touch and they'll build something around your schedule and goals.
