Common questions

Is the training plan really free?

Yes, no cost and no catch. Drop your name and email above and we'll send the PDF straight to your inbox.

Do I need to be an M2 member to use the plan?

No, the plan is free for anyone training toward HYROX Auckland. The free Saturday class is also open to non-members.

Which track should I follow, Beginner or Intermediate?

The plan explains how to pick based on your current running and training background, it's covered on page 2.

When do the Saturday HYROX classes start?

Late October. Keep an eye on @m2trainingclub or the class timetable for exact dates.

Can I get a personalised version of the plan?

Yes, Dave and Bekka both offer 1:1 and small-group HYROX coaching built around your own schedule and goals. Get in touch via the Become a PT / coaching enquiry page.
