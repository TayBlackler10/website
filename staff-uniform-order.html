Good to know

Common questions

How do I pay?
Build your cart, then tap checkout. It takes you to a secure Stripe checkout for your whole order, and you pay by card. Once it's paid you'll see a confirmation page, and an email with your order lands within about 10 minutes.
Where do my sizes and role go?
When you check out, your name, role and full order (sizes and colours included) are logged automatically. You don't need to add anything at Stripe, just pay.
What's branded and what's not?
Puffer vests, jackets, long sleeves, Lululemon tops and 1/4 zips get the M2 mark, printed in white, except the Lululemon tops (the reception top), which print green, and reception's 1/4 zips, which print grey. Shorts, leggings and socks are plain LSKD with no print.
When do I get it?
Unbranded pieces come through as normal store orders. Branded pieces go to print in batches. We'll message you when it's ready to collect at reception, 8 Nugent Street, Grafton.
How do I pick my size?
Tap Size guide under any product. Shorts and leggings open LSKD's fit guide, everything else opens the product page where the size chart sits. If you're between sizes, ask reception, some of the team have tried them on.
Wrong size or need to change something?
Let reception know as soon as you can, before anything goes to print.
Can I get a refund?
No refunds or returns once an order is placed. These are staff discount prices, so all sales are final.
