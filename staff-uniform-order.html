Common questions

How do I pay?

Build your cart, then hit checkout. It opens a secure Stripe checkout for your whole order, pay by card, same as the hoodie presale.

Where does my size and role go?

When you check out, your name, role, and full order (sizes and colours included) get logged automatically. You don't need to do anything extra at Stripe, just pay.

What's branded vs unbranded?

Puffer vests and jackets get the M2 mark printed in white. Long sleeves, shorts and socks are plain LSKD, no print.

When do I get it?

Unbranded pieces ship as normal LSKD orders. Branded pieces go to print in batches, we'll message you at pickup time. Collect at 8 Nugent Street, Grafton.

Wrong size or need to change something?

Reach out to reception as soon as you can, before anything's gone to print.

Can I get a refund?

No refunds or returns once an order is placed. These are staff discount prices, so all sales are final.
