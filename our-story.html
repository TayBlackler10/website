How it started

Built in Grafton

We opened our doors at 8 Nugent Street in Grafton in October 2023.

The idea was simple. Build a proper training club with the space, the equipment and the recovery to back it up, and fill it with coaches who actually care.

Three years on, more than 1,900 members train with us.

The name

What M2 stands for

M2 stands for Mindset × Movement. How you think shapes how you train, and how you train shapes who you become.

Progress isn't built by movement alone, or by mindset alone. It happens when both work together. When your mind gets stronger, your body follows.

That's what M2 is built on.

Our philosophy

Built on three things

01

Train with purpose

Every session has a reason. We train to get better, not just to get through it.

02

Progress with intention

Results come from showing up when it's hard, not just when it's easy.

03

Grow through community

The people around you matter. At M2 you train alongside people who take it seriously.
