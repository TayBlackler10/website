The Build

15 weeks, four phases

Counting back from Melbourne, the program runs Base → Threshold Build → Race-Specific Peak → Taper, then a short turnaround into Pro Doubles five days later. Every phase is built on the same principle: the engine comes first, specificity comes last.

WK 1–6BASE
WK 7–11BUILD
WK 12–14PEAK
WK 15TAPER
9 DECRACE
Aerobic base — Zone 2 volume, TT bike, general strength, weakness technique
Threshold build — tempo running, compromised running, strength-endurance
Peak — full simulations, race pacing, doubles-specific work
Taper — volume down, sharpness up
Why this order: HYROX is a threshold-paced event stacked on top of eight strength-endurance stations. Research across HYROX-specific coaching (Endurox, TrainRox, RoxZone, HyroxDataLab) converges on the same hierarchy — frequency and aerobic volume are the foundation, threshold work sits above it, and race-specific simulation only pays off once the base can support the fatigue it creates. Skipping straight to simulations before the engine is built is the most common way HYROX athletes plateau or break down.

Weekly shape
Frequency
Double days, most days
AM aerobic session (run or TT bike, 60 min floor in Base/Build), PM strength / skill / weakness work. Friday PM is always off — full rest, every week, no exceptions.
The bike
TT trainer carries volume
A meaningful share of Zone 2 work moves to the bike, especially in Base phase. It builds the aerobic engine without the impact cost of extra running mileage, and keeps legs fresher for strength and station work.
The weak points
Burpees & lunges get their own sessions
Dedicated "Weakness Lab" blocks (EMOM burpee broad jumps, loaded lunge ladders) run twice a week through Base and Build, then fold into compromised-running circuits as race day nears.
Weekends
Long aerobic + circuit/simulation
Saturday carries the long aerobic session (running or bike, alternating). Sunday shifts from an aerobic capacity circuit in Base phase to full or partial race simulations by Peak phase. From Week 3, Sunday PM adds a genuine 45-min AMRAP or EMOM conditioning test (alternating week to week) — real effort, not technique pace. Triple-session weekends appear from Week 10 onward on simulation days.
A note on recovery
Double and triple training days on top of a demanding training load put a premium on sleep, protein intake and total calories — this matters more than usual given the appetite suppression that comes with GLP-1/GIP-class compounds. Under-fuelling on a big volume week is the fastest way to blunt adaptation and stall recovery, so treat hitting your calorie and protein targets as part of the program, not an afterthought. This program covers training only — for anything specific to your TRT or retatrutide protocol, that's a conversation for your prescribing doctor, not this app.
