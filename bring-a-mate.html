How it works

Three easy steps

1 Tell your mateSend them this page, or just bring them in for a look.
2 They join and mention youAny membership. They just give your name at reception when they join.
3 You both get 4 weeks freeAnd your mate pays no joining fee or key tag fee, worth $79.
The fine print

Simple as that

No codes, no forms, no limit. Bring 5 mates and you get 20 weeks free.

  • You need to be a current M2 member.
  • Your mate needs to join on a membership. Trials don't count.
  • You both get 4 weeks free. Your mate also pays no joining fee or key tag fee, worth $79.
  • No codes. Your mate tells reception who referred them when they join.
  • Unlimited referrals. Every mate who joins gets you another 4 weeks free.
  • Fitness Passport memberships are excluded from all deals and offers.

Good to know

Common questions

Do I need a referral code?
No. There are no codes. Your mate just tells reception who referred them when they join.
Does it count if my mate does a trial?
No. Your mate needs to join on a membership for you both to get 4 weeks free. Trials don't count. See memberships
How many mates can I bring?
As many as you like. There's no limit. Bring 5 mates and you get 20 weeks free.
Can I use it with Fitness Passport?
No. Fitness Passport memberships are excluded from all deals and offers, including Bring a Mate.
