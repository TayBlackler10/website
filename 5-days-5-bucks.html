Common questions

How much does the trial cost?
Just $5 for the full 5 days. No lock-in and no ongoing commitment.
What's included?
Everything. The full gym floor, all group classes including HYROX, the 20m pool, spa pool, sauna and ice bath. View the timetable
When do my 5 days start?
Your 5 days run from the day you first check in, so you get the full window from your first session.
