Good to know

HYROX questions

Is M2 an official HYROX gym?
Yes. We're an official HYROX Training Club in Grafton, with HYROX classes, race-spec equipment and coaches who race.
What HYROX classes do you run?
HYROX Threshold, HYROX Engine and HYROX Teams. See the timetable
Do I need HYROX experience?
No. HYROX Threshold is open to all levels and you don't need any HYROX experience for HYROX Teams. Your coach will scale the session to where you're at.
Which memberships include HYROX classes?
M2 Classes ($35 a week) and Perform ($49.50 a week). Fitness Passport members get every class too. The Daily membership is gym floor only.
I'm racing HYROX Auckland. Is there a deal?
Yes. Show your HYROX Auckland race entry at reception and get Perform Flexi for $35 a week instead of $54.50, over 35% off. It's in person only. There's also a free 2 week plan. See the HYROX Auckland offer
