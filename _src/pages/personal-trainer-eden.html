About me

Grounded in hauora

Kia ora, I'm Eden. Wherever you're starting from, you're welcome here. I help you build strength at your own pace, walking alongside you while you grow.

My coaching is grounded in hauora, the belief that your physical, mental and spiritual wellbeing are all connected. Strength matters, but so does how you sleep, how you feel, and how you speak to yourself.

Every session is shaped around you, whether you're starting fresh, coming back after a break, or learning to train with your body and hormones rather than against them.

No judgement, no pressure, just steady progress you can actually keep. Book a free consultation, and let's take the first step together.
