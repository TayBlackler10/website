Getting the most from it

A simple way to use it

  • Straight after training. Get in the ice bath within 30 minutes of finishing.
  • Contrast rounds. 3 minutes ice bath, 10 minutes sauna. Repeat 2 to 3 rounds. Finish cold to recover, warm to relax.
  • Rest days. 20 to 30 minutes of easy swimming keeps things moving without adding load.
  • Be consistent. Two or three sessions a week makes a noticeable difference within a month or two.

Good to know

Recovery questions

Which memberships include recovery?
Perform ($49.50 a week) and Recovery ($35 a week) both include the ice bath, sauna, spa pool and 20m pool. Daily and M2 Classes memberships don't. Compare memberships
Do I need to book?
No. Everything is first come, first served during opening hours: Monday to Friday 5am to 10pm, and Saturday and Sunday 7am to 7pm.
How cold is the ice bath?
It's kept at around 8 to 12°C. Start with 2 to 3 minute sessions and build up gradually.
How should I use the ice bath and sauna together?
Try 3 minutes in the ice bath then 10 minutes in the sauna, and repeat 2 to 3 rounds. Finish cold for recovery or warm to relax.
