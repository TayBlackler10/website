Good to know

Common questions

Do I need a referral code?
No. There are no codes. Your mate just tells reception who referred them when they join.
Does it count if my mate does a trial?
No. Your mate needs to join on a membership for you both to get 4 weeks free. Trials don't count. See memberships
How many mates can I bring?
As many as you like. There's no limit. Bring 5 mates and you get 20 weeks free.
Can I use it with Fitness Passport?
No. Fitness Passport memberships are excluded from all deals and offers, including Bring a Mate.
