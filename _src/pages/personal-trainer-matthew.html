About me

Consistency over perfection

I spent years as a sparky, fitting training in around working full time. Early starts, long days and finding time for sessions taught me that you don't need a perfect schedule to make progress. You need a plan that fits the life you actually have.

Running and HYROX racing became my passion, and competing has shown me how much structure, consistency and smart pacing matter, both on race day and in everyday training. I bring that same approach into every programme I build.

My coaching is built on consistency over perfection. Whether you're chasing your first race, building strength, or creating sustainable habits to lose weight and feel better, I'll build a plan around your goals, your schedule and where you're starting from.
