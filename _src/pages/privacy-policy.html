Your data, your rights

Privacy policy.

M2 Training Club Limited ("M2", "we", "us"), 8 Nugent Street, Grafton, Auckland 1023. Email reception@m2club.co.nz.

We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 when we collect, use and look after your personal information.

What we collect

When you enquire, register for an event, start a trial or join M2, we may collect your name, email, phone number, date of birth, emergency contact and payment details, plus anything you tell us in a form or questionnaire (for example your training goals when you book a free PT session). If you train with us through Fitness Passport, Fitness Passport confirms your membership status with us.

How we use it

We use your information to manage your membership, take payments, run class and recovery bookings, reply to your enquiries, match you with a coach, and send you service updates. If you've agreed to it, we also send you news and offers by email or text. You can opt out at any time.

Website analytics, cookies and advertising

Our website uses a few tools that set cookies or similar technology in your browser:

  • Google Analytics. Helps us understand how people use the site, such as which pages are visited and which buttons are clicked (for example "start your trial" or "join"). This information is about how the site is used and isn't used to identify you personally.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram). Lets us measure how our ads perform and show relevant M2 ads to people who have visited our site. Meta may combine this with information it already holds about you, under Meta's own privacy policy.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

The M2 app

When you sign in to the M2 app with your GymMaster email and password, the app shows your membership, visits and class bookings from GymMaster. Anything you log yourself in the app, such as workouts, recovery sessions and food, stays on your phone and isn't sent to us. If you ask for a free PT session in the app, your answers go to our coaching team so we can match you with a trainer.

Location. When you tap Open door, the app checks your phone's location once to confirm you're at the club. It's only used for that check. We don't track your location and we don't keep it.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the app sets class reminders and the odd nudge on your phone. You can turn these off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Apple Health and Health Connect. If you connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), the app reads your steps, active energy, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and workouts so it can show them to you. You choose what to share on your phone's permission screen. This data is read on your phone only. It isn't sent to us or anyone else, it isn't used for advertising, and the app never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect. You can disconnect in the app, or remove access in your phone's Health or Health Connect settings.

Strava. If you connect Strava, you sign in on Strava's own page and choose to let M2 see your activities. We keep the link Strava gives us so the app can show your recent runs, rides and workouts (type, date, time, distance and heart rate). We only read your own activities, we don't post anything to Strava and other members never see them. Tap Disconnect Strava in the app and we delete that link straight away. You can also remove M2 in your Strava settings.

Deleting your account. You can ask us to delete your app account and data from the Me tab in the app (Delete my account), or by emailing reception@m2club.co.nz.

Who we share it with

We only share information with the services that help us run M2: GymMaster (memberships, billing, member emails, the M2 app, class bookings and door access), Technogym Mywellness (class timetable and bookings), Google (website analytics, and storing enquiries from some website forms), Web3Forms (delivering website form messages to us), Stripe (online merchandise payments), Meta (advertising) and Fitness Passport (for Passport members) and Strava (only if you connect it in the app). We don't sell your information.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and only keep it for as long as we need it. Some of the services above store information overseas, and we choose providers that protect it to a comparable standard.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing reception@m2club.co.nz. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email we send, or by replying to let us know.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
