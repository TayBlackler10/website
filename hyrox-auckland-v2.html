Common questions

Is the 2-week plan really free?

Yes, no cost and no catch. Drop your name and email above and we'll send the plan straight to your inbox.

Do I need to be an M2 member for the Saturday class or technique session?

No, both are free and open to non-members. You do need to book your spot via our Technogym app first.

How do I claim the HYROX Auckland membership discount?

Bring proof of your HYROX Auckland race entry to reception at 8 Nugent Street, Grafton. It's only available in person. It can't be claimed online.

What counts as proof of my race ticket?

Your HYROX Auckland registration confirmation, whether that's an email, a screenshot or your entry showing in the HYROX app.

Can I get a personalised training program instead of the free plan?

Yes. Dave and Bekka both offer online HYROX coaching at a discount for anyone racing HYROX Auckland. Enquire above and they'll build something around your own schedule and goals.
