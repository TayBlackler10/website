Gym near Auckland Hospital.

We're a short walk from Auckland City Hospital and the University's Grafton campus. Open from 5am on weekdays, with parking on site.

Fitness Passport

Work at the hospital? You may already be covered.

Lots of workplaces offer Fitness Passport as a staff benefit. If yours does, you get full access at M2: the gym, every class including HYROX, and the pool, spa, sauna and ice bath.

Check with your employer, or log in to your Fitness Passport portal to see if M2 is on your plan. Then just bring your card or app to reception. There's a one-off $25 key tag on your first visit.

Good to know

Common questions

Can I use Fitness Passport at M2?
Yes. Fitness Passport members get full access to the gym, every class including HYROX, and the recovery area. Check with your employer or log in to your Fitness Passport portal to see if M2 is on your plan. Fitness Passport at M2
How close is M2 to Auckland City Hospital?
We're at 8 Nugent Street, Grafton, a short walk from Auckland City Hospital and the University of Auckland Grafton campus. Get directions
What time do you open?
Monday to Friday 5am to 10pm, and Saturday and Sunday 7am to 7pm.
Is there parking?
Yes, there's parking on site. Grafton train station is a short walk away too.
Can I train after a night shift?
Yes. We open at 5am on weekdays and 7am on weekends, so you can come in after a night shift for a session, a swim or some time in the sauna and spa before you head home.
