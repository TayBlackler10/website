Our classes

Pick your class

Three HYROX formats, a strength class and yoga. All coached, all levels.

HYROX

HYROX Threshold

High intensity intervals built around the HYROX race format: rowing, ski erg, sled push and wall balls. Builds the power and muscular endurance you need on race day.

HYROX Strength

Strength work built for race day: sled push and pull, lunges, carries and wall balls, plus the lifts that make them feel easier.

HYROX Teams

Partner and crew workouts built around HYROX Doubles and Relay. Competitive, fun and a great way to get race ready. No HYROX experience needed.

Good to know

Class questions

Which memberships include classes?
M2 Classes ($35 a week) and Perform ($49.50 a week) both include unlimited classes, including HYROX. Fitness Passport members get classes too. The Daily membership is gym floor only and doesn't include classes. Compare memberships
Do I need to book?
Yes. Book your spot through the timetable above, which runs on Technogym's Mywellness, or in the Technogym app.
Are the classes OK for beginners?
Yes. HYROX Threshold and Strength Club are open to all levels, and you don't need any HYROX experience for HYROX Teams. Your coach will scale the session to where you're at.
When is yoga?
Coach Rhea runs Hatha yoga on Mondays at 6:30pm and Yin yoga on Wednesdays at 6:30pm.
Can I try a class before I join?
Yes. Our trial gives you full access to the club, classes included, so you can try a few before you pick a membership. Start your trial
