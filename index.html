Good to know

Common questions

Where is M2 Training Club?
We're at 8 Nugent Street, Grafton, a few minutes from the Auckland CBD. There's parking on site and Grafton train station is a short walk away. Get directions
How much is a gym membership?
From $27.50 a week for the gym floor. M2 Classes and Recovery are $35 a week, and Perform, which includes everything, is $49.50 a week. Compare memberships
Can I try before I join?
Yes. Our trial gives you full access to the gym, classes, HYROX training and the recovery area. Start your trial
Can I use Fitness Passport at M2?
Yes. Over 1,200 Fitness Passport members train with us. You get full access to the gym, every class including HYROX, and the recovery area for a one-off $25 key tag. How it works
What are your opening hours?
Monday to Friday 5am to 10pm, and Saturday and Sunday 7am to 7pm.
Is M2 an official HYROX Training Club?
Yes. We run HYROX classes with race-spec equipment and coaches who race. HYROX at M2
