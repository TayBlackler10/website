Common questions

Where is M2 Training Club?

We're at 8 Nugent Street, Grafton, Auckland 1023 — a few minutes from the Auckland CBD. Parking on site. Get directions →

How much is a gym membership?

Memberships start from $27.50/week for gym floor access. Perform (gym, classes, HYROX, pool, sauna, ice bath) is $49.50/week. See all memberships →

Is M2 an official HYROX Training Club?

Yes. M2 is an official HYROX Training Club in Auckland. HYROX programming, expert coaching and race-spec equipment are included in Perform at no extra charge.

Can I try before I commit?

Yes — free for 3 days, full access to everything. Come in and see for yourself. Get your trial →

What are your opening hours?

Monday–Friday 5:00am–10:00pm and Saturday–Sunday 7:00am–7:00pm. Call 09 558 1408 or email reception@m2club.co.nz.

Do you have parking?

Yes, parking on site. We're also a short walk from Grafton train station and easy to get to by bus from the CBD.
