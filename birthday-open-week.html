Good to know

Common questions

Is it really free?
Yes. No card needed. Just register, then come in any day from 12 to 18 October.
Do I need to book classes?
Yes. Book through the Technogym app. Spots are limited because classes fill up, so get in early. View the timetable
What do I bring?
A towel, a water bottle and your training gear. There's parking on site at 8 Nugent Street, Grafton. Get directions
Can I bring a friend?
Yes, bring them along. They just need to register too.
I'm already a member?
Nothing changes for you. Just train as normal.
