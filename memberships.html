Good to know

Membership questions

How much is a gym membership at M2?
Daily is $27.50 a week for the gym floor. M2 Classes is $35 a week for the gym plus unlimited classes and HYROX. Recovery is $35 a week for the pool, spa, sauna and ice bath. Perform is $49.50 a week and includes everything.
How long is the membership term?
Daily is a 12 month term. M2 Classes, Perform and Recovery are 6 month terms. If you'd rather not commit, go Flexi.
What is Flexi?
Flexi is the same membership with no fixed term. It's $5 a week extra on any membership and you can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice.
Are there any other fees?
There's a one-off $49 joining fee and a $25 key tag. Both are free when you join on Perform.
Do I get a free PT session?
Yes. Every new member gets one free PT session with one of our coaches, except on Recovery memberships. Book your free PT session
Which memberships include the pool, sauna and ice bath?
Perform and Recovery. Both include the 20m pool, spa pool, sauna and ice bath. Perform also includes the gym floor and unlimited classes. See the recovery area
Can I pay for a year upfront?
Yes. Perform Annual is $1,800 and Daily Annual is $1,215, paid upfront. Spots are limited. Annual memberships
Can I use Fitness Passport at M2?
Yes. M2 is a registered Fitness Passport facility. Fitness Passport members get Perform access for a one-off $25 key tag fee. Our deals and offers aren't available to Fitness Passport members. Fitness Passport at M2
