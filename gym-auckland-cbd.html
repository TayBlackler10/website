Good to know

Common questions

How far is M2 from the CBD?
We're at 8 Nugent Street, Grafton, a few minutes from the Auckland CBD. It's easy to get to by car, bus or train. Get directions
Is there parking?
Yes, there's parking on site.
Can I get there by public transport?
Yes. Grafton train station is a short walk away, and it's an easy bus trip from the CBD.
What time do you open?
Monday to Friday 5am to 10pm, and Saturday and Sunday 7am to 7pm. Plenty of time to train before or after work.
How much is a membership?
From $27.50 a week for the gym floor. M2 Classes and Recovery are $35 a week, and Perform, which includes everything, is $49.50 a week. Compare memberships
Can I use Fitness Passport?
Yes. Fitness Passport members get full access to the gym, every class including HYROX, and the recovery area. Fitness Passport at M2
