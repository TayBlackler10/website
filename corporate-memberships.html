Why teams train at M2

One club, everything in it

A full gym, classes and recovery at 8 Nugent Street, Grafton.

Whole club

All under one roof

Gym floor, classes, HYROX, a 20m pool, spa pool, sauna and ice bath.

Easy to get to

Before work, after work

Parking on site and a short walk from Grafton station. Open Mon to Fri 5am to 10pm, Sat and Sun 7am to 7pm.

Recovery and physio

Look after your body

Pool, spa, sauna and ice bath, plus in-house physio from Prana Physio. There's a cafe on site too.

Coaching and classes

Real coaches

HYROX, Strength Club and yoga, run by our coaches. We're an Official HYROX Training Club.
