Privacy Policy.

M2 Training Club Limited ("M2", "we", "us")
8 Nugent Street, Grafton, Auckland 1023
reception@m2club.co.nz

We comply with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 when handling your personal information.

What we collect

When you enquire, trial, or join M2, we may collect your name, email, phone number, date of birth, emergency contact, and payment details. If you join via Fitness Passport, your Passport provider shares your membership status with us to confirm access.

How we use it

We use your information to manage your membership, process payments, run our recovery suite and class bookings, and send you service updates. With your consent, we also send marketing emails (via Mailchimp) and may show you ads on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram) based on your interaction with our website or app.

Sharing

We share information with the service providers that help us run M2: GymMaster (membership management), Mailchimp (email), Fitness Passport (for Passport members), and Meta (for advertising). We don't sell your information to third parties.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing reception@m2club.co.nz. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any email we send.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
