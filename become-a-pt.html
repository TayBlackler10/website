M2 Training Club provides a PT course designed by trainers, for trainers, for the best possible chance of success in the fitness industry.

Graduates employed (or contracted) by M2 will have covered all aspects of the NZQA Level 4 Qualification in Personal Training, and will be enrolled to complete their certificate.

Why study here

Real-World Experience

You're learning inside a real, active commercial gym, not a classroom. From week one you're coaching real members.

Flexible Study

Online theory at your own pace, combined with in-person practical sessions at M2. Built to fit around full-time work.

Job Pathway

Top graduates have a real pathway onto the M2 coaching team. We hire from within.
