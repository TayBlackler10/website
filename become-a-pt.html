The course

Turn your passion into a career

Graduates employed or contracted by M2 will have covered all aspects of the NZQA Level 4 qualification in personal training, and will be enrolled to complete their certificate.

Why study at M2

Learn on a real gym floor

  • Real-world experience. You learn inside a busy commercial gym, not a classroom. From week one you're coaching real members.
  • Flexible study. Online theory at your own pace, plus in-person practical sessions at M2. Built to fit around full-time work.
  • Payment plans. $3,000 total with 25% off (normally $4,000), paid weekly, fortnightly or monthly to suit your budget.
  • A job pathway. Top graduates have a real pathway onto the M2 coaching team. We hire from within.
  • REPs recognised. The NZ Certificate in Exercise Level 4 lets you register with REPs, opening doors in NZ and overseas.
