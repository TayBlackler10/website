What You'll Cover

Course Breakdown

The Level 4 qualification covers everything from anatomy and programme design to business skills and client management.

Theory Modules

  • Anatomy, physiology & biomechanics
  • Nutrition fundamentals for exercise
  • Health screening & risk stratification
  • Exercise prescription & programming
  • Special populations (injury, pre/post-natal, older adults)
  • Business skills — marketing, client management

Practical Assessments

  • 1:1 client coaching sessions
