Key principles for Avalon:
Zone 2 is conversational — if you can't hold a full sentence, slow down. Strength sessions are non-negotiable through Phase 1 and 2. Nutrition practice on rides over 90min starts Week 9 — target 60–90g carbs/hr. Recovery weeks are training — do not add sessions. The full simulation in Week 30 is your most important session. Taper is normal — feeling fresh by Week 38 is the goal, not the problem.
