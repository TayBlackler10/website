Fitness Passport at M2.

Show your card and get full Perform access at M2 in Grafton. Gym, classes, HYROX and recovery. Just a one-off $25 key tag.

How to use your passport Fitness Passport website
What you get

Our top membership, included.

Fitness Passport members at M2 get Perform, our full access membership, normally $49.50 a week. Same floor, same classes, same recovery area as every other member.

  • Full gym floor. Free weights, barbells, cables and cardio. No booking needed.
  • Unlimited classes. HYROX Threshold, Strength, Teams and Strength Club.
  • 20m pool and spa pool. For laps, active recovery or winding down.
  • Sauna and ice bath. The ice bath sits at 8 to 12°C.
  • Genuinely unlimited. No visit caps, no class limits, no blacked-out peak hours.
First time here?

How to use your passport at M2

No pre-registration and no separate booking system. You'll be training within minutes.

1Check you're eligibleLog in at portal.fitnesspassport.co.nz to confirm M2 is on your employer's plan and your passport is active.
2Get your cardSign up through your employer's Fitness Passport portal. You'll get a card, app access or both.
3Visit M2Come to 8 Nugent Street, Grafton and show your card or app at reception. A one-off $25 key tag fee applies on your first visit.
4Train and recoverThe gym, classes and recovery area are all yours. Book classes through our timetable.

Heads up: Fitness Passport members are excluded from all M2 deals and offers.

Good to know

Fitness Passport questions

Does my Fitness Passport work at M2?
Yes. M2 Training Club is a registered Fitness Passport facility. Log in to your Fitness Passport portal to confirm M2 is included in your employer's plan.
What do Fitness Passport members get at M2?
Full Perform membership benefits: the gym floor, unlimited group classes including HYROX, and the full recovery area with the ice bath, sauna, spa pool and 20m pool.
Is access really unlimited?
Yes. No visit caps, no class limits and no blacked-out peak hours. Train, take classes and use the recovery area as often as you like, whenever we're open.
Is there any cost for Fitness Passport members?
Just a one-off $25 key tag fee on your first visit. After that, the gym, classes and recovery area are all included.
Do I need to register before my first visit?
No. Just come in with your card or app and we'll check you in at reception.
Can Fitness Passport members use M2's deals and offers?
No. Fitness Passport members are excluded from all M2 deals and offers.
My employer doesn't offer Fitness Passport. Can I still join M2?
Yes. M2 memberships start from $27.50 a week, and every membership has a Flexi option. See memberships
