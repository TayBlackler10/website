How to use your passport at M2

First time here?

1
Step 01
Check Eligibility

Log in at portal.fitnesspassport.co.nz to confirm M2 is on your employer's plan and your passport is active.

2
Step 02
Get Your Card

Sign up through your employer's Fitness Passport portal. You'll receive a card and/or app access.

3
Step 03
Visit M2

Come to 8 Nugent Street, Grafton. Show your card or app at reception — we'll check you in. A one-off $25 key tag fee applies on your first visit.

4
Step 04
Train & Recover

Full gym access, classes and all recovery facilities are yours. Book classes through our timetable.

Fitness Passport FAQs

What benefits do Fitness Passport members get at M2?

Full Perform membership benefits — the complete package. Gym floor access, unlimited group classes, plus the full recovery area: ice bath, sauna, spa pool and 20m pool.

Is access really unlimited, or are there caps and blackout times?

Genuinely unlimited. No visit caps, no class limits, no blacked-out peak hours. Train, take classes and use the recovery area as often as you like, whenever we're open.

Is there any cost for Fitness Passport members?

Just a one-off $25 key tag fee when you first visit. After that, everything — gym, classes and the full recovery area — is included at no extra cost.

Does my Fitness Passport work at M2?

Yes — M2 Training Club is a registered Fitness Passport facility. Log into your portal to confirm M2 is included in your employer's plan.

Do I need to register before my first visit?

No pre-registration required. Just show up with your card or app and we'll check you in.

My employer doesn't offer Fitness Passport. Can I still join M2?

Absolutely. M2 memberships start from $27.50/week with flexible options. See memberships →
